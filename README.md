## 🧑‍💻 About Me

> *"Engineering scalable systems. Extracting meaning from data. Building the future — one commit at a time."*

I'm a **Software Engineering student at SLIIT (Sri Lanka Institute of Information Technology)**, driven by a passion for architecting elegant, data-powered solutions at scale. My flagship project, the **Smart University Platform**, is an integrated ecosystem designed to solve real campus-life challenges — from intelligent parking management to a peer-to-peer marketplace. I thrive at the intersection of **full-stack engineering and data science**, transforming raw datasets into actionable, business-critical insights that drive smarter decisions.
